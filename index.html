Capability and data limits

The inspected files contain no sensor ingestion, PUE calculation runtime, anomaly detector, forecasting model or alert delivery. No measured efficiency or carbon reduction is established.

Use synthetic or public data. This page has no upload, account connection, live monitoring, automatic alerts or AI execution. It does not provide legal advice, safety certification or a guaranteed outcome.

A human-owned review example

  1. Use fictional readings: total facility energy 120 kWh and IT equipment energy 100 kWh for the same interval.
  2. A human reviewer can calculate illustrative PUE as 120 ÷ 100 = 1.20; this page does not calculate or validate it.
  3. Record the meter boundary, interval, data quality and facilities owner before considering any operational change.
